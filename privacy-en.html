Privacy Policy

Bible Heart · Last updated: November 10, 2026

Bible Heart (“we”, “the app”) is published by Viggo Aarslev, France. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and how you can delete it. In short: we collect only what the app needs to work, we never sell your data, we show no ads, and we do not track you across other apps.

1. What we collect

DataWhy
Your first nameTo personalise your daily prayers.
Your quiz answers: what you are going through, your familiarity with the Bible, your Christian tradition (optional), your reminder timeTo choose your journeys and verses, and to send your daily reminder. Your Christian tradition is a religious belief: you share it only if you choose to answer.
Your progress: days completed, streak, bookmarksTo show your progress and keep your streak.
Messages you send to the AI companion (“Ask” tab), and its repliesTo answer you and keep your conversation history.
Your subscription statusTo unlock Premium features.
Usage statistics: screens and features used (e.g. “session completed”, “paywall viewed”)To understand what helps people and improve the app. These never include your first name or the content of your messages.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifier. The app creates an anonymous account for you: no email address or password is required.

2. The AI companion

When you write in the “Ask” tab, your message, your first name, your language and what you said you are going through are sent to Anthropic (the company behind the Claude AI model) to generate a reply. Anthropic processes this data on our behalf and does not use it to train its models. The AI can make mistakes; it is not a pastor, a doctor or a therapist.

To keep the companion safe and accurate, we review a sample of conversations from which names, email addresses and phone numbers have been removed. If a message suggests someone may be in danger, the app shows crisis helplines; we do not contact anyone on your behalf.

4. How long we keep it

Your data is kept while you use the app. You can delete it at any time (see below). Anonymised conversation samples used for quality review are kept for up to 12 months. Usage statistics are kept for up to 24 months.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything: in the app, go to Profile → “Delete my data”. Your account, profile, progress and conversations are permanently erased from your phone and our servers.
  • Notifications: you can turn reminders off at any time in your phone’s settings.
  • Subscription: cancel at any time in your App Store or Google Play settings. Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription.
  • Depending on where you live (for example in the European Union, the United Kingdom or California), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to its processing. Write to us at bible.heartt@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days.

Legal bases (EU/UK): performing our service to you (account, progress, companion, subscription), your explicit consent for the optional Christian tradition answer, and our legitimate interest in improving the app (usage statistics). We do not sell or share your personal information as defined by California law.

6. Children

Bible Heart is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the data.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and protected by access rules so that each account can only read its own data.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
